QORIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

QORIQ is a digital legacy protocol demonstration project. This policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on your use of the application, information may include account name, email address, authentication credentials, guardian and heir details, digital will information and audit events.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide account access, manage digital legacy demonstrations, support guardian verification and maintain security records.

4. Security

QORIQ uses security measures such as password hashing and authenticated access. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

Google services may be used for email delivery. Their processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

6. Demonstration Disclaimer

QORIQ is a demonstration project. Do not submit real private keys, financial credentials or other highly sensitive information. Actual asset transfers are not enabled.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact qoriq.knq@gmail.com.
